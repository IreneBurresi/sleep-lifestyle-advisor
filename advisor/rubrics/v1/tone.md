Tone: plain, second person, calm, no jargon, no alarm, no flattery.

Score from 1 to 5:
5 = plain words a non-specialist reads once, second person, no exclamation marks, no medical or statistical jargon, no praise for its own sake
4 = one jargon term ("cardiovascular", "standard deviation", "metrics") or one stiff phrase
3 = two or more jargon terms, or a sentence that recites the person's data instead of talking to them
2 = alarmist or moralising ("you must", "dangerous", "you need to take this seriously"), or flattering filler
1 = not addressed to the person, or unreadable

Examples, unrelated to the case you are grading:
- "You sleep less than most people in your group. A ten minute walk after lunch is an easy place to start." Score 5.
- "Your cardiovascular metrics sit 1.2 SD above the cohort mean, so it is imperative you act now." Score 2.
