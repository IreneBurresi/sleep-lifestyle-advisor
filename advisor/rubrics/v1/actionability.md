Actionability: the person can start each recommendation this week.

Score from 1 to 5:
5 = every recommendation names one concrete action with a when or a how much ("a ten minute walk after lunch on three days")
4 = one recommendation is concrete but has no when or how much
3 = one recommendation is a restatement of a goal ("improve your sleep quality") or of the current state ("keep doing what you do")
2 = half or more of the recommendations are goals or restatements
1 = nothing the person could do differently tomorrow

Examples, unrelated to the case you are grading:
- "Turn off work notifications one hour before bed." "Walk ten minutes after lunch on workdays." "Take five slow breaths before each meeting." Score 5.
- "Focus on refining your rest." "Maintain your current stress level by continuing whatever works." "Add a short walk." Score 2.

Answer with the integer score as the score field.
